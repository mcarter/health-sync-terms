Carter Personal Health Sync

Terms of Use and Privacy Notice

What this app is

Carter Personal Health Sync is a personal, single-user tool. It lets one individual — the person who installs and runs it — download a copy of their own health record from their healthcare provider's patient portal and keep that copy on their own computer.

It is not a commercial service. There is no company behind it, no user accounts, no servers operated by the developer, and no other users.

What data it accesses

After you sign in to your patient portal and explicitly authorize access, the app reads your own health records through your provider's standard patient-access FHIR APIs. This may include allergies, conditions, medications, lab results, vital signs, immunizations, procedures, encounters, care plans, care team, clinical notes and reports, imaging reports, insurance coverage, and related demographic information.

The app is read-only. It never writes to, modifies, or deletes anything in your medical record.

Where your data goes

  • Your health data is stored only on your own device, in a local database file and local attachment files that you control.
  • Your data is never transmitted to the developer, to any hosted service, or to any third party.
  • Your data is never sold, licensed, shared, or used for advertising, marketing, research, or analytics.
  • No one other than you has access to it. There is no staff, no partner, and no affiliate with access.

Access tokens and credentials

The app never sees or stores your patient portal username or password — you enter those directly on your provider's own login page. The app stores only the access and refresh tokens your provider issues, in a file on your device with owner-only permissions.

Your control

  • Complete copy: all data the app has stored is in your local database, and the app can export all of it at any time.
  • Deletion: you can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the app's local data directory. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that deletion is complete.
  • Revoking access: you can revoke the app's authorization at any time from your patient portal's "Linked Apps and Devices" page. Revoking access immediately stops any further syncing.
  • Retention: the local copy is kept for as long as you choose to keep it. The developer retains nothing, because the developer receives nothing.

Security

Data is stored on your device and is protected by your device's own security (for example, full-disk encryption and your account password). Because the data is a copy of your medical record, you should keep device encryption enabled and avoid placing the data directory in shared or cloud-synced locations unless you intend to.

No medical advice, no warranty

This app copies and stores records; it does not interpret them and does not provide medical advice. It is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. Your provider's patient portal remains the authoritative source for your health record. Do not rely on this local copy for clinical decisions.

Business associate status

The developer is not a HIPAA business associate of any healthcare provider and has no business associate agreement with any covered entity. The app operates solely under the individual's own right of access to their health information.
